Two causes and their outcomes

Shared parser

Track the parser correction in issue #1.

Day-first tenants pass dates to a shared month-first parser. An app booking for 7 April can become an invite for 4 July. Parse the explicit tenant format and normalize the stored date. Recheck app, email, and booking-v2 flows. Different subject lines can still describe this cause. Engineering reports the parser fix complete; this review does not claim to have deployed or independently verified that fix.

booking-v3 hint

Track the form hint correction in issue #2.

The form prints MM/DD/YYYY while reading dates in the day-first tenant order. A customer who follows the hint for 1 June can receive 6 January. Generate the displayed hint and input parsing from the same tenant format. The affected path is the form for booking-v3 tenants; fixing the shared parser alone does not remove this contradiction. Engineering retired booking-v3 instead of changing this hint, so its closure is not planned rather than completed.
